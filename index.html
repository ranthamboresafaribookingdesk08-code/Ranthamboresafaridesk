Explore Sawai Madhopur

Top Places to Visit in Sawai Madhopur

Apart from the tiger safari, Sawai Madhopur offers rich heritage, ancient temples, and picturesque lakes for travelers.

Ranthambore Fort

Located inside the national park, this 10th-century UNESCO World Heritage site offers majestic views of the entire tiger reserve forest.

Trinetra Ganesh Temple

Situated atop Ranthambore Fort, this famous 3-eyed Lord Ganesha temple is one of the oldest and most revered shrines in Rajasthan.

Surwal Lake & Bird Watching

A serene seasonal lake near Sawai Madhopur, perfect for morning sunrises and observing migratory birds like flamingos and cranes.

Jogi Mahal & Banyan Tree

An ancient royal hunting lodge situated on Padam Talao lake, home to India's second largest iconic Banyan Tree.

Icons of the Jungle

Famous Tigers Of Ranthambore

1. MACHALI (T-16)
Machali, the most famous tigress of Ranthambore, was known as the "Queen of Ranthambore". Her iconic battle with a 14-foot crocodile remains legendary.
2. SUNDARI (T-17)
Daughter of Machali, Sundari ruled the lakes area of Ranthambore for years and was widely photographed by wildlife enthusiasts worldwide.
3. ARROWHEAD (T-84)
Granddaughter of Machali, Arrowhead is named after the arrow-shaped mark on her left cheek and dominates the core lake zone.
